Our Story

Who We Are

A non-profit voluntary society born out of a commitment to social justice — serving marginalised communities across rural Kurnool District since June 1993.

Our Vision

To be a sustainable organisation empowering the rural poor — economically and socially — irrespective of caste and creed, so that every individual can live with dignity, opportunity, and hope.

Our Mission

To enable the rural poor to attain sustainable growth by creating livelihood opportunities through microfinance and income-generation programs via a fair, transparent, and community-driven system.

Our History

1993

Foundation & Registration

Dalit Welfare Association formally registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 (Reg No. 384/1993) through the District Registrar of Assurances, Kurnool, on June 22, 1993.

2004 – 2012

Grassroots Campaigns

Launched adult literacy drives, solid waste management programmes, safe drinking water initiatives, and community hygiene campaigns across Kurnool District villages.

2016 – 2017

First Major International Grant

Executed the first overseas-funded project — a dairy farming & livestock programme funded by Global Compassion Inc. (USA) with a grant of ₹4,39,000, directly benefiting BPL families.

2020 – 2022

COVID-19 Emergency Relief

Distributed ₹8.32 Lakh in emergency relief (funded by Divya Foundation) — 30 kg dry ration kits and youth happiness kits — to BPL families and street children during the pandemic.

2024 – 2025

Women's Banking & Entrepreneurship Programme

Launched the flagship 12-month programme targeting 50 rural women entrepreneurs in Sirivella Block. Funded by Grace TPTC Foundation via an FCRA grant of ₹33,19,167 — the largest programme in DWA's history.

Core Mandate & Principles

No Profit Motive

Operates strictly without any profit motive or commercial profit-sharing under registered bylaws.

Non-Political

Prohibits political activities, agitation, or partisan engagement of any kind.

No Office Salaries

Office bearers receive no salary or dividends from society funds — fully volunteer-led governance.

Inclusive Service

Serves all communities irrespective of caste, creed or religion with equal dignity.
