DIMENSION 01 · THE PROBLEM

Structural Marginalization & Agrarian Crisis in Rural Rayalaseema

In the semi-arid Rayalaseema belt of Kurnool District—specifically Sirivella Block, Bandi Atmakur Mandal, and surrounding rural Panchayats—marginalized Dalit families face compound historical inequities and severe climatic vulnerability. For generations, restricted land ownership and absence of formal credit forced communities into low-wage, unorganized agricultural labor. Coupled with recurring drought cycles, groundwater depletion, and El Niño monsoon failures, rain-fed agrarian distress drives crop loss, extreme household debt, and seasonal distress migration.

DIMENSION 02 · THE PLAN

Our Roots: Thirty Years of Community Dignity & Collective Action

In June 1993, in Parnapalli village of drought-prone Rayalaseema, grassroots social workers gathered with a foundational conviction: true transformation cannot be imposed from outside—it must be built from within. To break cycles of caste inequity and unorganized debt traps, the community established the Dalit Welfare Association (DWA), chartered on June 22, 1993 under Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 under the enduring motto: "Stronger Together." Operating with an uncompromising zero-profit charter and 100% honorary governance, DWA mobilizes sustainable grassroots self-reliance.

LEGAL & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JANUARY 2026

The Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is firmly committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of donors, beneficiaries, volunteers, and website visitors.

1. Information Collection

We only collect personal information (name, email address, postal address, PAN number for 80G tax receipts, phone number) when voluntarily submitted by donors or newsletter subscribers.

2. Use of Information

Collected details are strictly used to issue official statutory tax-deductible receipts, comply with Government of India (Income Tax Dept & Ministry of Home Affairs FCRA) regulations, and deliver requested program updates. DWA never sells, trades, rents, or exchanges donor data with third parties.

3. Data Security

All donation transactions processed through accredited portals (Give.do, Benevity) employ industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We maintain appropriate physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards.

4. Contact Officer

For any queries or data removal requests, please write to our Project Officer at dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com.

DONOR ASSURANCE · STATUTORY REFUND

Donation Refund & Cancellation Policy

LAST UPDATED: JANUARY 2026

The Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is a non-profit voluntary society registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860. All contributions directly support charitable programs for rural poverty alleviation, women’s livelihood, and child education.

1. Voluntary Contributions

Donations once made are voluntary and non-refundable once statutory tax exemption certificates (80G receipts or FCRA returns) are generated.

2. Erroneous or Duplicate Donations

If an erroneous deduction, technical duplicate transaction, or accidental charge occurs, the donor must notify DWA within 7 working days of the transaction date. Please submit the transaction reference, payment screenshot, donor name, and banking proof to dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com.

3. Refund Processing

Upon verification, approved refunds will be credited back to the original source account/card via the banking channel within 10 to 14 business days, in compliance with applicable banking and RBI regulations.
