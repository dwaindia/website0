Livelihood Initiative

Women's Banking & Enterprise

Women's Banking, SHG Micro-Credit & Livelihood Enterprise Development

Methodology & Impact

By organizing rural women into democratic Self-Help Groups (SHGs), DWA breaks the dependency on predatory informal moneylenders who charge usurious interest rates up to 60-120% annually. Our groups practice disciplined weekly savings, receive zero-interest revolving seed capital, and obtain direct linkage to commercial banks under government priority sector schemes.

Program Pillars

SHG Microfinance Clusters

Federations of 10-15 women with internal rotating loan funds managed through transparent, community-audited passbooks.

Vocational Tailoring Units

State-of-the-art sewing machine training centers producing school uniforms, blouses, and market garments for commercial retail.

Livestock & Dairy Assets

Providing high-yield milch buffaloes, sheep units, and poultry pens with veterinary healthcare insurance support.

Digital Financial Literacy

Hands-on smartphone training on UPI, mobile banking, digital passbooks, and government direct benefit transfers (DBT).

Direct Market Linkage & Fair Trade

Eliminating middlemen by connecting women producers directly to regional textile markets and consumer cooperatives.

Education Initiative

Dalit Child Education Support

Dalit Child Education Support: Retention, Evening Tutoring & Literacy Centers

Methodology & Impact

Without academic reinforcement at home due to first-generation learner dynamics, rural Dalit children face immense grade repetition and dropout risks. DWA’s village evening learning centers bridge this learning divide, providing dedicated teacher guidance, homework support, and nutritional snacks.

Program Pillars

Daily Evening Tutoring

2 hours of structured coaching every evening in Mathematics, Science, Telugu, and English led by qualified community educators.

Free School Kits & Books

Annual distribution of high-grade backpacks, notebooks, geometry sets, shoes, and uniforms to eliminate financial barriers to schooling.

Girl Child Retention Drive

Targeted counseling, adolescent hygiene kits, and parent meetings ensuring adolescent girls transition from middle school to higher secondary.

Higher Secondary Stipends

Direct fee assistance and bus pass subsidies for meritorious underprivileged students entering polytechnic, nursing, and intermediate colleges.

Digital Literacy Classrooms & STEM

Interactive tablet and computer-aided learning modules introducing basic coding, keyboard mastery, and digital research tools.

Relief Initiative

Emergency & Seasonal Relief

Emergency Response, Seasonal Drought Relief & Crisis Food Security

Methodology & Impact

Rayalaseema experiences recurring climatic stress where groundwater tables drop below 800 feet. In times of drought or flash monsoon floods, daily-wage laborers lose income immediately. DWA maintains an emergency standby fund that mobilizes drinking water tankers and dry rations within 24 hours of notification.

Relief Pillars

Drinking Water Tankers

Daily clean water tankers dispatched directly to water-starved Dalit colonies during peak summer heat waves (March–June).

Dry Ration & Grain Kits

Essential kits containing 25kg rice, pulses, cooking oil, spices, and salt sustaining destitute families through periods of agricultural wage collapse.

Seasonal Winter Blankets

Warm thermal blankets and woolen clothing distributed to elderly widows, orphans, and thatched-hut dwellers across rural Panchayats.

Mobile Health & Medical Camps

Free medical consultations, diagnostic testing, and vital medicines distributed in partnership with volunteer doctors.

Flood & Disaster Rebuilding Assistance

Tarpaulins, building materials, and emergency cash assistance helping families restore damaged roof tiles and dwelling walls.

Residential Care

Orphanage Home & Shelter

Orphanage Home, Child Protection & Comprehensive Residential Care

Methodology & Impact

For children who have lost one or both parents to agrarian distress, tuberculosis, or extreme poverty, DWA provides a safe, nurturing residential home. Children receive full room and board, balanced three-course meals, routine health monitoring, and enrollment in reputable English medium schools.

Care Pillars

24/7 Secure Residential Home

Spacious, clean dormitories equipped with personal study desks, beds, and full-time resident wardens and matrons.

Nutritious Diet & Paediatric Care

Balanced nutrition including milk, eggs, fresh vegetables, seasonal fruit, and monthly growth assessments by a paediatrician.

Formal English Medium Schooling

Tuition fees, uniforms, and textbooks covered 100% to ensure equal access to quality formal education.

Psychosocial Counseling & Joy

Trauma-informed counselors helping children overcome grief through art therapy, sports, cultural dance, and educational excursions.

Vocational Skills & Independent Living Prep

Career counseling, computer hardware coaching, and life-skills workshops preparing older youth for successful adult independence.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is firmly committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of donors, beneficiaries, volunteers, and website visitors.

1. Information Collection

We only collect personal information (name, email address, postal address, PAN number for 80G tax receipts, phone number) when voluntarily submitted by donors or newsletter subscribers.

2. Use of Information

Collected details are strictly used to issue official statutory tax-deductible receipts, comply with Government of India (Income Tax Dept & Ministry of Home Affairs FCRA) regulations, and deliver requested program updates. DWA never sells, trades, rents, or exchanges donor data with third parties.

3. Data Security

All donation transactions processed through accredited portals (Give.do, Benevity) employ industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We maintain appropriate physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards.

4. Contact Officer

For any queries or data removal requests, please write to our Project Officer at dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com.

Donation Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is a non-profit voluntary society registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860. All contributions directly support charitable programs for rural poverty alleviation, women’s livelihood, and child education.

1. Voluntary Contributions

Donations once made are voluntary and non-refundable once statutory tax exemption certificates (80G receipts or FCRA returns) are generated.

2. Erroneous or Duplicate Donations

If an erroneous deduction, technical duplicate transaction, or accidental charge occurs, the donor must notify DWA within 7 working days of the transaction date. Please submit the transaction reference, payment screenshot, donor name, and banking proof to dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com.

3. Refund Processing

Upon verification, approved refunds will be credited back to the original source account/card via the banking channel within 10 to 14 business days, in compliance with applicable banking and RBI regulations.
