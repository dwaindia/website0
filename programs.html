What We Do

Our Programs

Three core intervention areas — livelihood, education, and emergency relief — targeting the most vulnerable families across Kurnool District.

Flagship Programme · 2024–2025

Women's Banking &
Entrepreneurship Development

A 12-month intervention targeting 50 rural women entrepreneurs (prioritising BPL, single, and widowed women aged 25–50) across 3 villages in Sirivella Block, Kurnool District. Funded by Grace TPTC Foundation via an FCRA grant of ₹33,19,167.

6-Stage Operating Model

  1. 1.
    Livelihood Scoping — Market research mapping local demand, value chains, and raw material availability.
  2. 2.
    Beneficiary Selection — Community meetings with Self-Help Groups to select 50 high-interest women.
  3. 3.
    Curriculum Customisation — Dialect-translated workbooks on financial literacy and trade skills.
  4. 4.
    Resource Deployment — Master Trainers deployed for hands-on instruction.
  5. 5.
    3-Phase Skill Building — Financial education & digital banking; business planning; livelihood upgradation (tailoring, livestock, micro-retail).
  6. 6.
    Monitoring & Evaluation — Baseline and post-intervention tracking of income and savings.

Target KPIs

  • +35% revenue growth per beneficiary
  • +25% formal savings growth
  • 100% bank accounts with debit cards
  • ≥60% Udyog Aadhaar registration
  • ≥50% active UPI/digital payment adoption

Ongoing Programme

Dalit Child Education Support

Academic outreach across 10 Government Primary Schools in Sirivella and Bandi Atmakur mandals — ensuring underprivileged Dalit children stay in school and receive quality educational resources.

School Supply Kits

₹1,200 per kit — includes 3 long notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, colour pencils, 1 durable school bag, and 1 new uniform/dress set.

Merit Scholarships

Direct financial aid awarded annually to high-performing Dalit students to prevent dropout and encourage continued academic excellence.

10 Govt. Schools Covered

Active outreach in Sirivella and Bandi Atmakur mandals, ensuring every enrolled child has access to quality learning materials.

Emergency Programme

Emergency & Seasonal Relief

Rapid-response distribution of food rations and welfare kits to BPL families during crises — including the COVID-19 pandemic response in FY 2020–21.

COVID-19 Relief (FY 2020–21)

Supported by a ₹8,32,500 grant from Divya Foundation. Distributed 30 kg dry ration kits and youth happiness kits to BPL families and street children across Kurnool District.
